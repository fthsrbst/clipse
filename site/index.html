          01
          
It forgets nothing.

          

            No cap, no eviction, no "last 20 items". The history is a file on your machine, indexed
            for full-text search, and it stays until you delete it.
          

        


        

          02
          
Except what it must.

          

            Copy a password out of your password manager and Clipse does not store it. Not hidden,
            not encrypted — never written. Same for API keys, card numbers and private keys. It is
            enforced in the capture path, before the history exists.
          

        


        

          03
          
Your devices. Nobody else's.

          

            Pairing takes a one-time six-digit code you confirm on both screens. Devices that never
            exchanged one cannot see your clipboard, cannot ask for it, and cannot join by being on
            the same network.
